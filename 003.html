Breed Info -- Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

The Cavalier is gentle, affectionate, and known for its expressive eyes and loving nature.

Temperament

Friendly, gentle, loyal, and excellent with families.

Grooming

Regular brushing, ear care, and coat trimming recommended, including sanitary and comfort trims.

Colours

Golden, black, roan, parti, chocolate, sable, and more.

ANKC Breed Standards

Official DogsWest and ANKC standards for the breed.

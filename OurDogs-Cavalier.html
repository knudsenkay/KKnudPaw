Cavalier King Charles Spaniel

The Cavalier is gentle, affectionate, and known for its expressive eyes and loving nature.

Temperament

Sweet, cuddly, and excellent with children.

Grooming

Regular brushing and ear care recommended.

Colours

Blenheim, tricolour, ruby, black & tan.

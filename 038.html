Training

The dogs I get into training are all rescues of differant breeds who can show potential to become one of these types of service dogs.

• Autism Assistance Dogs

• PTSD Assistance Dogs

• Physical Assistance Dogs

• Purposeful Placement

• Facility Support

I only have a max of 2 dogs in training at a time, so I can dovote the time they require to first get over their past, then learn the required actions they will use the rest of their working lives.

I then put them through the PAT Test which is required for all service dogs.

Breed Info

Learn about the temperament, grooming needs, colours, and breed standards of the Spaniels we raise.

Temperament

Spaniels are affectionate, gentle, eager to please, and thrive in family environments.

Grooming

Regular brushing, ear care, and coat maintenance are essential for keeping your Spaniel healthy.

Colours

Spaniels come in a wide range of colours including golden, black, roan, parti, chocolate, sable, and more.

ANKC Standards

We follow DogsWest and ANKC breed standards to ensure ethical, responsible breeding.

Puppy Care Guide

Everything you need to know to give your new puppy the best start in life.

Feeding

Feed a high-quality puppy food recommended by your breeder. Keep meals consistent and avoid sudden diet changes.

Sleep & Routine

Puppies thrive on routine. Provide a quiet, safe sleeping area and maintain consistent wake, play, and rest times.

Socialisation

Introduce your puppy to new people, sounds, environments, and gentle experiences to build confidence.

Training

Use positive reinforcement. Start with simple cues like sit, stay, and recall. Keep sessions short and fun.

Grooming

Brush regularly, check ears weekly, and introduce grooming early so your puppy becomes comfortable with handling.

Vet Care

Follow your vet’s vaccination schedule, worming plan, and health checks. Keep records organised.

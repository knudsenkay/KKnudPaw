Clumber Spaniel

The Clumber Spaniel is calm, dignified, and known for its gentle nature and distinctive heavy build.

Temperament

Quiet, affectionate, and very loyal.

Grooming

Regular brushing and ear care required.

Colours

White with lemon or orange markings.

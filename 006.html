Breed Info

Springer Spaniel

The Springer Spaniel is energetic, athletic, and highly trainable -- perfect for active families.

Temperament

Friendly, gentle, loyal, and excellent with families.

Grooming

Regular brushing, ear care, and coat trimming recommended -- including sanitary, comfort, and hand-strip options.

Colours

Liver and white, black and white, or either of these colours with tan markings.

ANKC Breed Standards

Official DogsWest and ANKC standards for the breed.

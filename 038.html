Training

The dogs I get into training are all rescues of different breeds who can show potential to become one of these types of service dogs.

  • Autism Assistance Dogs
  • PTSD Assistance Dogs
  • Physical Assistance Dogs
  • Purposeful Placement
  • Facility Support

I only have a maximum of 2 dogs in training at a time, so I can devote the time they require to first get over their past, then learn the required actions they will use for the rest of their working lives.

I then put them through the PAT Test, which is required for all service dogs.

Then they are ready for their new home.

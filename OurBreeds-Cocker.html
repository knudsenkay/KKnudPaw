Cocker Spaniel Breed Standard

Last updated: 09 Oct 2012

English Cocker Spaniel

The English Cocker Spaniel is affectionate, intelligent, eager to please, and known for its beautiful silky coat.

Temperament

Friendly, gentle, loyal, and excellent with families.

Grooming

Regular brushing, ear care, and coat trimming recommended.

Colours

Golden, black, roan, parti, chocolate, sable, and more.
